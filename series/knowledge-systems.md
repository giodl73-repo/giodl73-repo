# Knowledge Systems Series

This series builds large human-readable corpora and analytical systems with
explicit review gates. The subject matter changes, but the contract is the
same: make a serious body of knowledge, connect conclusions to evidence, then
keep it honest with rubrics, panels, provenance, and executable validation.

| Repo | Role |
|------|------|
| [LUCIA](https://github.com/giodl73-repo/LUCIA) | Human chronicle: cultures told from inside their own worldview. |
| [MAXIM](https://github.com/giodl73-repo/MAXIM) | Peer-level reference library across computing, science, arts, and material culture. |
| [CERES](https://github.com/giodl73-repo/CERES) | Local production atlas for evaluating modern artisan-production equipment and civic-scale viability. |
| [TAXLANE](https://github.com/giodl73-repo/TAXLANE) | Evidence-gated federal spending and income-tax analysis: fifteen tracks, one accounting spine, and a rate result that refuses unsupported savings. |

## TAXLANE highlight

TAXLANE reverses the usual fiscal-policy workflow. It does not start with a
preferred tax rate or a percentage cut. It asks what public services cost,
tests candidate spending changes against accounting, source, beneficiary,
continuity, distribution, overlap, and implementation gates, and only then
models the ordinary-income rate needed for the scoped remainder.

Its current internal result is:

- fifteen tracks examined;
- ten reviewed-zero spending dispositions;
- **$0.000B** admitted FY2026 primary spending reduction;
- **$813.727B** scoped remaining ordinary-income target; and
- preferred analytical marginal schedule **21/23/33/35/43/46/48**, with
  separate contingency and severe-stress rails.

The deeper contribution is the accounting spine. Program owners book supported
net effects; PAY measures without double counting; NET follows an admitted debt
path; OAS remains a separate solvency overlay; and REV finances the scoped
remainder. A failed gate produces a typed zero rather than invented savings.

The final explanation program contains 21 canonical deliverables, ten papers,
three decks, a local static website, machine records, two `.roles` rounds per
wave, and 388 passing tests. Start at the
[TAXLANE repository](https://github.com/giodl73-repo/TAXLANE) or its
[briefing bundle](https://github.com/giodl73-repo/TAXLANE/blob/main/docs/explanation/final/briefing-bundle-index.md).

The result is analytical and repository-contained. It is not enacted law,
personal advice, an official score, formal proof of balance, or an authorized
deployment.

## Why these belong together

These are corpus projects, but not merely collections of files. They are
reviewed bodies of knowledge with gates, style contracts, evidence trails, and,
where appropriate, executable tests that stop a simplified story from outrunning
its sources.
